JEV APPROACH CONTROL

A small air traffic control sim for runway 27. Give radio calls in plain English, or hand the frequency to Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, and watch it sequence, space and land the traffic.

How it works ↗ · Source on GitHub · by Zeb88 · unofficial demo, not affiliated with TypeSafe AI
Landed: 0 · Lost: 0
Runway 27 (land westbound). Vector arrivals onto the final approach course east of the runway at or below 3000 ft (30° intercept or less), clear them for the ILS approach, then clear them to land before 1.5 mi or they go around. Keep 1000 ft or 2 miles apart. Or hand the frequency to Jev.
